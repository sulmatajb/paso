# Roadmap

Paso is a working beta. These are contribution priorities, not delivery commitments.

## Available today

- Five Spanish beginner units with 25 lessons; French and English starter lessons.
- Lesson progression, placement, mistake practice, FSRS reviews, and local persistence.
- Account sync with Sites-hosted authentication and D1.
- Optional Whisper/Qwen/Kokoro conversations through free quota-limited Hugging Face hosting, your own server, or capable local devices.
- Six voice choices, bundled lesson audio, and a responsive PWA shell.

## Highest priority

1. **Learning quality:** independent educator/fluent-speaker review, accepted-answer coverage, consistent regional pronunciation, and documented learning goals.
2. **Real phones:** iOS/Android microphone and playback tests, slow connections, low memory, interrupted sessions, and accessible controls.
3. **Portable accounts:** a documented, tested production identity adapter outside Sites; account deletion and retention controls; explicit backup restore.
4. **More complete courses:** expand French and English using the existing content schema, then evaluate additional language pairs with reviewers and licensed voices.
5. **Reliable conversations:** human-speech benchmarks, clearer feedback grounded in lessons, latency/capacity measurements, and robust free-quota handling.

## Useful small contributions

- Reproduce and document a phone-specific bug without including private recordings.
- Review one lesson and submit a focused correction with language rationale.
- Improve a keyboard/focus interaction or a 320-pixel layout.
- Try the setup from a fresh clone and improve a confusing step.

Open an issue before starting a large feature. We do not currently claim fluency certification, a complete curriculum, unlimited free inference, or a finished generic hosting platform.
